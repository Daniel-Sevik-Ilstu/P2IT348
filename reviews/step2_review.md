## What I checked and found correct

- KS is run per feature (`ks_2samp` per column) between two year samples, for all 28 pairs. It is not pooled across features and is not tested against a fixed year only.
- Constant-in-both features are skipped and counted (`n_skipped`), and their NaN values never reach `bonferroni_significant` as significant. `m` excludes skipped features, as PLAN says. A NaN guard raises on any tested feature.
- Years and labels come from the `year` and `label` columns of the loaded frame. The features list is `feature_columns(df)` (1,848 names). Sampling is class-balanced with `random_state=42`.
- The top features and years that drift most are reported: `step2_top20_features.csv` (top 20 by mean KS vs 2014) and `step2_ks_all_pairs.csv` (ranked pairs, 2014-2021 largest).
- No leakage into later steps: nothing in drift.py is imported by the modelling code, and the single-year fits are covered by the PLAN section 0 exemption. Label mapping (1 = malware) is not used in the KS part. The matrix uses accuracy only, which PLAN specifies for Step 2.
